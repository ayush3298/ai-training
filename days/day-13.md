## Goal
Learn the adaptation ladder (prompt → RAG → fine-tune) and what fine-tuning really is, so you pick the right rung.

## Why today matters
Most teams reach for fine-tuning when a prompt or RAG would do. Knowing the ladder saves weeks and a training bill you don't need.
